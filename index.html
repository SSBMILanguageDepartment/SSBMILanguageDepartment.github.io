Welcome to the Shingle Springs Band of Miwok Indians Language Department's website!

Here, you can find resources that the SSBMI Language Department has created for learning Nisenáan (Nisenan) and Míw’a (Northern Sierra Miwok), including self-paced language lessons, audio recordings, and other materials. Please check out the links below to learn more!

Nisenan Bingo rules and language

The SSBMI Language and Tribal Services departments co-host periodic Bingo events, where we play a custom version of Bingo to practice Nisenan language vocabulary. So far, we have done versions of the game that focus on animal names and Halloween-related vocabulary.

Click here to learn more about Nisenan Bingo, including how to play and practice language. To learn more about events for SSBMI Tribal Members, email us at Language@ssband.org.

Language Day Camp - July 28, 2026

The SSBMI Language Department hosted the fifth Language Day Camp in July 2026, where we played a version of Nisenan-language Bingo with the Tribal Youth to learn animal names and celebrate the end of the summer. Participants received a new woolém eepén (honeybee) pin.

Click here to learn more about Language Day Camp and find other resources.

Folsom Zoo Language Trip - May 15, 2026

The SSBMI Language Department hosted the fourth Folsom Zoo Language Trip in May 2026, where we visited the Folsom City Zoo Sanctuary with SSBMI Tribal Members & TANF families while learning Nisenan and Northern Sierra Miwok language for talking about the animals.

Participants received a new set of flashcards and a custom weekwék (peregrine falcon) pin. They learned from activities hosted by the SSBMI Environmental and Language departments, while SSBMI Tribal Police assisted with temporary language tattoos.

Click here to learn more about this year's language outing; you can listen to audio recordings of the Nisenan and Northern Sierra Miwok language that we shared at the zoo.

Koymóon C’aan Ha (Rattlesnakes & Logs)

The SSBMI Language Department created a new language game called Koymóon C’aan Ha, which is a Nisenan-language version of Snakes and Ladders. This game is fun for all ages and perfect for learning to count in Nisenan.

"May" storybook

In December 2025, the SSBMI Language Department published a new storybook, "May". In this story, we learn from the Tribe's relation Tom Cleanso how may (salmon) got the orange-red color of its meat and the river began to move during Creation Time.

This is the second storybook in the SSBMI Nisenan Storytelling Collection, our collaboration with the SSBMI Exhibits & Collections Center's Lólmen Publications.

Click here to learn more about the storybook. There, you can listen to the story in Nisenan and English, access the digital version of the storybook, and view more artwork.

Shingle Springs Rancheria plant signage

The SSBMI Language Department, Environmental Department, and our 2025 Summer Youth Interns created plant signage sharing the Nisenan names of some of the plants found around the Rancheria. You can find plant signage near the Tribal Admin building and in other places.

Each sign has a companion webpage where you can listen to the name of the plant and learn more about it, including about its traditional uses and other information. You can learn more about the project and find a searchable list of Nisenan plant names here.

SSBMI Greetings Initiative

The SSBMI Language Department has asked all staff to learn greetings in the Tribe's heritage languages (one Nisenan and one Northern Sierra Miwok), and we are encouraging them to use these greetings in their employment capacities as part of our Greetings Initiative.

Greetings are fun and easy to learn and use. Using Tribal language greetings in the workplace helps show that this is a Tribal community that values its heritage languages while reinforcing that this is a safe space for learners to use their language. Whether you are an SSBMI Tribal Member and/or Tribal employee, we hope you will join us in using these greetings.

Click here to learn different ways you can say "hello" and "goodbye" in Nisenan and Miwok.

Nisenan and Miwok "Go Fish!" cards

The SSBMI Language Department created custom "Go Fish!" cards for learning Nisenan and Miwok animal names and other language for talking about "having" and "giving".

Our "Welk’óy!" (Nisenan) and "Wélse’!" (Miwok) cards feature illustrations of 32 local animals along with their Nisenan/Miwok names. If you would like a copy, please visit our offices in the Tribal Admin building.

Click here to learn more, including to listen to audio recordings of the Nisenan & Miwok language included on our "Go Fish!" cards and other language for playing "Go Fish!" in Nisenan and Miwok.

Menekɨ́m siyéebe hedém website-í? (Who makes this website?)

This website is maintained by SSBMI Language Department staff in order to share Nisenan and Miwok language learning resources with the Tribal Membership and other learners. Our current team consists of: Jonathan Geary, Richenda Ervin, and Skye Anderson. The Nisenan and Miwok audio recordings that you will find on this website are recordings of Jonathan, Richenda, and Skye. We are all learners of the Nisenan and Miwok languages, and we hope that you will join us in learning Nisenan and Northern Sierra Miwok too!

The SSBMI Language Department is part of the Cultural Resources Division of the Shingle Springs Band of Miwok Indians. We host Nisenan and Miwok language classes and other language-focused events, create Nisenan and Miwok learning resources (e.g. language games, books, activity sheets, and self-paced learning materials), and share Nisenan and Miwok language information via other means such as an ongoing social media campaign (please follow the Cultural Resources Division on Facebook!). If you would like to know more about our efforts, or if you have any questions about the Nisenan and/or Miwok languages, please contact us at Language@ssband.org.

Wenném! Sitapáy’omís! (Thank you! Take care of yourself!)
